Flight Manual & Operational Protocols

Mastering core navigation, landing deck etiquette, and supercruise dynamics prevents multi-million credit rebuy screen disasters.

Rule #1
CRITICAL

Never fly without your Rebuy. Always keep enough liquid Credits to cover your insurance deductible (5% of total ship value). No exceptions.

Rule #2
POWER

4 PIPs to SYS grants 2.4x shield strength. When taking fire or bracing for impact, redirect power instantly to Systems.

Rule #3
NAVIGATION

High-Wake ignores Mass Lock. Jumping to another star system cannot be blocked by nearby heavy enemy ships, unlike supercruise Low-Wakes.

Core Navigation Beats

01 · The 7-Second Supercruise Approach Rule

When approaching stations or planets in Supercruise:

  • Set throttle to 100% until the countdown timer reaches 0:07 seconds.
  • Immediately drop throttle to 75% (the center of the blue band).
  • If done correctly, your speed will throttle down in perfect ratio to the destination's gravitational well, preventing overshooting.
02 · Fuel Scooping Protocol (KGBFOAM Rule)

Only main-sequence stars yield scoopable hydrogen fuel. Remember the acronym KGBFOAM.

Galaxy Map Tip: Go to Galaxy Map -> Map Filters -> Star Class -> Check K, G, B, F, O, A, M. Check "Apply Filter to Route".

Approach the star's upper atmosphere outside the yellow Exclusion Zone line. Maintain heat under 80% while harvesting.

03 · Neutron Star Jet Cone Supercharging

Supercharges your Frame Shift Drive, multiplying jump range by 400% for one jump:

  1. Equip a Fuel Scoop.
  2. Fly into the outer cone fringe facing away from the neutron star core.
  3. Ride the turbulence until the HUD flashes "FSD Operating Beyond Safety Limits".
  4. Vector out smoothly and execute your mega-jump.

Module Rating Golden Rules
OUTFITTING 101

Module letter classes represent specific trade-offs across all modules:

  • A-Rated: Peak performance, highest efficiency, heavy power draw.
  • B-Rated: Highest integrity/armored (heavy mass, durable).
  • C-Rated: Best cost-to-performance balance.
  • D-Rated: Ultra-lightweight (essential for exploration builds to maximize jump range).
  • E-Rated: Stock budget modules (replace these immediately).
