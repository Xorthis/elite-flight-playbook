02 · FLIGHT BEATS

Nine Core Pilot Maneuvers

Follow the sequence. Execute with discipline in every system arrival and departure.

1

Station Launch & Mailbox

Navigating station rotational gravity

Request docking clearance within 7.5km. Align with the center of the green light side of the slot. Retract landing gear immediately to clear mass lock. Flight Cue: Keep velocity under 100 m/s in station space to avoid reckless flying fines.
2

The 7-Second Supercruise Rule

Approaching stations without overshooting

Approach target at full throttle. When time-to-destination reaches 0:07 seconds, pull throttle back into the center of the blue band (75% power). Flight Cue: Never let the timer drop below 0:06 or you will loop past the gravity well.
3

Fuel Scooping & Heat Management

Harvesting main sequence stars

Only scoop KGBFOAM class stars. Skirt the corona until rate caps out; balance scoop yield against heat accumulation. Flight Cue: Keep heat below 80%. If heat exceeds 100%, throttle down and vector away immediately.
4

Interdiction Escape

Evading hostile tethering

Center throttle in the blue band. Roll ship to line up horizontal thrusters with the shifting Escape Vector escape ring. Flight Cue: In heavy cargo haulers, submit to interdiction immediately (throttle 0%) to get a fast 5-second FSD cooldown.
5

Neutron Jet Cone Boost

Supercharging your jump range by 400%

Equip an Fuel Scoop. Fly into the outer tail edge of a Neutron star's cone, facing away from the star core. Hold position until FSD is supercharged. Flight Cue: NEVER enter a White Dwarf cone unless experienced; stay away from the exclusion zone.

04 · COMMAND BANK

Quick Reference Guides

KGBFOAM Star Types (Scoopable)

Remember the acronym KGBFOAM (K, G, B, F, O, A, M). These are the only main-sequence stars that yield scoopable hydrogen fuel. Set your Galaxy Map route filter to these types only.

Power Distribution (PIP Allocation)

4 SYS: Shield damage resistance increased by ~2.4x.
4 ENG: Maximum agility, pitch rate, and boost replenishment.
4 WEP: Drains capacitor slower, allowing continuous laser fire.

Modules Priority Management

Set your Frame Shift Drive, Fuel Scoop, and Cargo Rack to Priority 2 or 3. Keep Thrusters, Shields, and Sensors on Priority 1 so your ship stays operational if your Power Plant drops below 100% capacity.
